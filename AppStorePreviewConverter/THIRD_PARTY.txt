Third-party components bundled with App Store Preview Converter
================================================================

This target may ship executable binaries under:

  AppStorePreviewConverter/Binaries/ffmpeg
  AppStorePreviewConverter/Binaries/ffprobe

The copies currently committed in this repository were produced from FFmpeg
upstream and configured approximately as follows (verify on your machine with
`./Binaries/ffmpeg -version` — the authoritative record is whatever that command
prints for *your* build):

  FFmpeg version reported: N-124300-gdba0b078c8
  Compiler: Apple clang

  Configure flags include:
    --enable-static --disable-shared
    --pkg-config-flags=--static
    --enable-gpl
    --enable-libx264

  (plus local --prefix/--extra-cflags/--extra-ldflags paths used on the builder’s
  machine — see full `configuration:` line from `ffmpeg -version`.)


1) FFmpeg
----------

Project: FFmpeg — https://ffmpeg.org/

Upstream states that most FFmpeg source files are under the GNU LGPL v2.1 or
later. Optional parts are GPL; enabling them changes the licensing of binaries
built from FFmpeg. Specifically, passing `--enable-gpl` activates GPL-covered
parts, and FFmpeg’s license for the resulting build is GPLv2-or-later unless you
explicitly opted into GPLv3-related settings as documented upstream.

Official references:
  Legal overview — https://www.ffmpeg.org/legal.html
  License text aggregation — https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/blob/master/LICENSE.md


2) x264 (via --enable-libx264)
-------------------------------

Building FFmpeg with `--enable-libx264` links against libx264, which is GPLv2+
software. Combined with FFmpeg’s `--enable-gpl`, the resulting FFmpeg binary is
distribution under GPLv2-compatible terms — not “LGPL-only.”

Upstream x264 licensing information is documented with the libx264 project you
compiled against.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

(This is informational, not legal advice.)

 • If you give others the ffmpeg/ffprobe binaries in this repository (or inside
   a built .app), you must follow the **GPL** (and any other applicable)
   obligations for those binaries: correct license notices, offering or
   providing corresponding source for FFmpeg and libx264 in the form required
   by the license, etc.

 • The **Swift application source** in this repository is a separate work for
   licensing purposes. If you redistribute the app **with** these GPL-enabled
   FFmpeg binaries, your overall distribution often must be planned so the
   combination remains compliant (commonly: release your app under a
   GPL-compatible license, or replace these binaries with a build that matches
   your intended license model — e.g. LGPL-only FFmpeg without GPL encoders).

 • Do not describe this bundle as “LGPL-only FFmpeg” — the shipped configuration
   above is **GPL-class** because of `--enable-gpl` and libx264.

 • Follow FFmpeg’s trademark / branding guidance when mentioning FFmpeg in
   marketing or UI (see https://www.ffmpeg.org/legal.html).

---------------------------

Copyright licenses (GPL/LGPL) are separate from codec patent pools or other

5) ffprobe
----------

`ffprobe` is built from the same FFmpeg sources and inherits the same license
characteristics as the FFmpeg build it came from.
